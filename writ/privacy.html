Privacy Policy for Writ

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Writ is designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect, store, use, or share any personal data or usage data from users of the app.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal information, identifiers, analytics, location data, contacts, or any other user data. Writ operates on your Mac and your documents stay on your device. There is one feature that can send part of a document to a server, and only if you switch it on yourself and tell Writ which server to use — see Optional diagram rendering below.

Third-Party Services

Writ does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or external tracking services that collect user data. Markdown, math, Mermaid diagrams and syntax highlighting are all rendered on your Mac by libraries bundled inside the app: nothing is downloaded at runtime and none of your content is sent anywhere.

Optional Diagram Rendering (PlantUML)

Writ can also render PlantUML diagrams. Unlike everything above, PlantUML has no bundled renderer, so this is the one feature that sends content off your Mac — and it is off unless you enable it twice over. The document must declare plantuml: true in its front matter, and you must enter the address of a PlantUML or Kroki server in Writ's settings. No server address is configured out of the box, and with none configured nothing is sent.

When both of those are in place, Writ sends the text of each PlantUML code block — not the rest of your document — to the server address you entered, and shows the diagram it returns. That server is one you choose and control, typically one you run yourself on your own machine or your own network. We do not operate it and never receive the request. The address you configure is stored only on your Mac.

Clearing the server address, or removing the plantuml: true line, stops Writ sending anything.

Data Sharing

Because we do not collect user data, we do not sell, share, or disclose user data to any third parties.

Children's Privacy

Writ does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the support method listed on Writ's product page.
